About

One kettle. One turntable. No small talk.

TeaTime started in a bedroom with two decks and a mixer that overheated after forty minutes — about as long as it takes a proper brew to go cold. What began as an inside joke between friends turned into a residency, then a road trip, then this.

The sets hit like water just off the boil: relentless four-on-the-floor, driving kicks, and Tekno rhythms that don't let up. Expect hard techno pushed to the edge, dropped without warning, for a crowd that didn't plan on dancing tonight.
